# Reading list — when challenged in the room

The skill works without finishing these. Open this file when someone disputes the form, or when the user asks for odds of various portfolio loss levels.

## Start here

| Pointer | Read when you need… |
| --- | --- |
| Hubbard, *How to Measure Anything* — Ch. 3–4 | Definitions of **measurement**, **uncertainty**, and **risk** (risk = uncertainty with quantified loss) |
| Hubbard, *How to Measure Anything* — Ch. 5 | Why experts give **90% confidence intervals** / calibrated probabilities; overconfidence |
| Hubbard, *How to Measure Anything* — Ch. 6 | What “measuring risk” means (odds of various loss levels); **how not** to measure risk (H/M/L, 1–5) |
| Hubbard, *How to Measure Anything* — Ch. 7 | **Expected opportunity loss** and value of information — measure what changes the decision |
| HDR litmus / cybersecurity webinars | The actuarial question: *What is the probability of losing more than X in the next 12 months due to Y?* — [Feb 2022 slides](https://hubbardresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/HTMA-CR-Webinar-February-2022-Final.pdf) |
| [One-for-One Substitution Model](https://hubbardresearch.com/one-for-one-substitution-model/) | Replacing a heat-map cell with **probability over a period** + **90% CI on impact** (and optional Monte Carlo → loss exceedance) |
